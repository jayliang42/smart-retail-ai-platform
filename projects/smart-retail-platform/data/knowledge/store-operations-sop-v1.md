# Store Operations SOP

## Inventory discrepancy

When shelf inventory differs from the system quantity, recount the sellable units and check the
receiving area before changing the system record. Record the reason, operator identity, and evidence.
An adjustment above 20 units requires manager approval. Never use an inventory adjustment to hide a
receiving or theft investigation.

## Stockout response

For a confirmed stockout, check backroom inventory and open deliveries first. If no units are
available, add the item to the replenishment exception queue and verify the next delivery date.
Do not change price automatically in response to a stockout.

## Price change control

Price changes must reference an active promotion or an approved pricing ticket. A price decrease
above 10 percent or any change affecting regulated items requires approval from the pricing lead.

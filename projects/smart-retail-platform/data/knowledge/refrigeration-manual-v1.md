# Refrigeration Unit Manual

## Normal temperature

The dairy case should operate between 1°C and 4°C. A single reading above 4°C should be verified
against a second sensor or a calibrated handheld thermometer before escalating.

## High-temperature alarm

If verified product temperature remains above 5°C for 15 minutes, move exposed product to a safe
case, notify the store manager, and open a priority-one refrigeration work order. Do not repeatedly
power-cycle the controller. Record sensor readings, door state, and compressor status.

## Sensor fault

A missing or impossible reading may indicate a sensor fault rather than a cooling failure. Inspect
the cable and compare another sensor before moving product. Replace a damaged sensor under the
device maintenance procedure.
